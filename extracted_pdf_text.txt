MAGICK – Core Definition
MAGICK is artificially induced genetic mutation, deliberately engineered and stratified by the great 
houses and the French crown. It is not a natural evolution or random gift. It is a controlled technology 
of the body, framed and policed so that it reinforces the existing hierarchy rather than threatening it.
Nature of MAGICK
It functions like X-Men-style mutations: the body is rewritten at the genetic and epigenetic level 
to produce superhuman abilities (enhanced strength, regeneration, sensory dominance, limited 
environmental manipulation, accelerated healing, etc.).
Unlike natural mutation, every package is designed, manufactured, and administered. There is 
no “wild” MAGICK that simply appears. What looks spontaneous is almost always the delayed 
expression of an earlier, hidden intervention (as with the hero).
Stability, potency, and inheritance are strictly graded:
Pureblood royal packages (royal + royal): the only form that is naturally inheritable across 
generations. Clean, powerful, and politically legitimized.
High noble packages: strong but usually require reinforcement or are only partially heritable.
Knight / retainer packages: functional combat and enforcement tools, rarely passed on 
cleanly.
Serf packages: narrow, productivity-focused (endurance, toxin resistance, rapid recovery 
from labor, sometimes heightened pain tolerance or compliance). Explicitly non-inheritable 
and non-threatening. Must be re-dosed each generation.
Lower ranks cannot simply “awaken” high MAGICK. The yearly collections and forced injections exist 
precisely because the hierarchy refuses to let stable power exist outside pure bloodlines.
Church framing and control
The Church does not reject MAGICK. It claims ownership of its meaning.
Official doctrine: MAGICK is a divine endowment mediated through legitimate blood and 
sacramental authority. The purest expressions are signs of God’s favor upon the rightful order. 
The dosing of serfs is presented as a merciful “gift of strength for honest labor.”
The Church maintains its own gene-foundries and inquisitorial auditors. They decide which 
packages are “orthodox” and which are heretical or demonic.
Any MAGICK that appears outside approved channels (especially in serfs or mixed-blood 
individuals) is declared either a miracle requiring investigation or a corruption requiring 
purification/excision.
Technological language is deliberately suppressed in public. The Church prefers terms such as 
“blood grace,” “sacred endowment,” or “the Mark of Station.” Speaking of MAGICK as mere 
genetic engineering is treated as dangerous skepticism or outright heresy.
This allows the Church to remain the moral and ritual authority while still fully participating in (and 
profiting from) the system.
Skepticism toward “tech”
Openly technological framing is politically toxic.
The great houses and the Crown present MAGICK as an extension of ancient blood right, not as 
laboratory product.
Independent medical centres, unlicensed gene-work, or talk of “engineering” are associated 
with the pre-collapse world that “failed.” The destruction of the centre that enabled the hero’s 
origin is both practical cover-up and ideological statement.
Skeptics who insist MAGICK is just advanced biotechnology are marginalized, accused of 
undermining the sacred hierarchy, or quietly removed.
In private, of course, the houses run highly sophisticated laboratories. The public face remains 
theological and hierarchical.
Practical consequences for the setting
A person’s legal and social value is partly measured by the grade of MAGICK they carry or are 
permitted to receive.
Detection systems at checkpoints, coming-of-age rites, and yearly collections constantly sort 
bodies according to the approved hierarchy.
Unlicensed or mismatched MAGICK (exactly what the hero carries) is both a practical threat and 
a theological scandal.
The Church’s blessing is required for high-grade packages; without it, even a powerful ability 
can be declared illegitimate.
